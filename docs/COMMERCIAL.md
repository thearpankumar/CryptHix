## When you need a commercial licence

You need one if any of these apply:

- You want to embed or bundle CryptHix, or code derived from it, in software you do not release under the AGPL.
- You run a modified CryptHix as a hosted or network service and do not want to publish your source code.
- Your organisation's policy does not allow AGPL-licensed software.

If you only use CryptHix unmodified, or you are happy to publish your changes under the AGPL, you do not need a commercial licence.
